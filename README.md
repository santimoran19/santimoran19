## 🧑‍💻 About Me

I'm a **final-year Systems Analysis student** in Córdoba, Argentina, graduating in **February 2027**.

Alongside my studies I've spent **3+ years in the IT department of the Argentine Army's 2nd Division**, managing infrastructure for 200+ users: LAN administration, Windows Server with Active Directory, DNS and domain services, and hardware diagnostics. That's where I learned to debug under pressure, with real people waiting on the other side.

I build software too — mostly full stack, with React, Next.js, TypeScript, Node.js and .NET. The combination is the point: **I resolve the incidents and I build the tools that prevent them.**

- 💼 Open to **junior developer** and **IT support** roles
- 🌍 Available for **remote work** — full overlap with US time zones (GMT-3)
- 🌱 Currently going deeper into **TypeScript**, **Next.js** and **cloud infrastructure**
- 🗣️ Spanish (native) · English (B1, actively advancing to B2) · Russian (A2)
